```
The lexicon distinguishes functions of language, not just meanings.

Movement verbs are structured along three axes:
- direction (toward)
- trajectory (movement)
- boundary (into/out of)

Communication verbs are structured as:
- content (λέγω)
- speech act (λαλέω)
- instruction (διδάσκω)
- proclamation (κηρύσσω)
```
