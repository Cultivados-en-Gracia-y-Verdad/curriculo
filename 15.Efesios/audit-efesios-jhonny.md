# Audit — “efesios-jhonny.md”

## Goal
Flag content that goes beyond observation (i.e., theological teaching, interpretation/inference, and application/exhortation).

## High-level result
The manual is **heavily interpretive and instructional** throughout (definitions, doctrinal framing, prescriptions like “debemos/no debemos”, and theological system claims). It is **not observation-only** in its current form.

## What to change (rule of thumb)

- Replace “**X significa / se refiere / esto muestra / por lo tanto** …” with **observable features**: grammar markers, discourse connectors, referents explicitly stated in the text, repeated lexemes, quoted phrases, etc.
- Move doctrinal/system frames (e.g., “Dios Trino”, “justificación/santificación”, “rapto”) into a separate **“Theology/Implications”** appendix if you still want them, but keep the main exposition observation-first.
- Convert imperatives (“no debemos…”, “deberíamos…”) into either: (a) **quoted commands** that are in the passage, or (b) remove them until an observation trail supports them.
