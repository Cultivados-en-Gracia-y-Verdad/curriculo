# A Huge Observation

The goal of Ephesians 4 is not:

### More leaders

### More converts

### More programs

### Bigger numbers

Those things may occur.

But the stated goal is:

```
Fullness of Christ.
```

That deserves to be near the center of the manual.

---

# Now Let's Return To Matthew 28

Suppose:

### Make disciples

is not the end.

Suppose it serves:

### Fullness of Christ.

Then:

```
Make disciples↓Equip saints↓Body grows↓Fullness of Christ↓Bride prepared
```

That would unite:

- Matthew 28
- Ephesians 4
- Colossians 1
- Ephesians 5
- Revelation 19

into one story.
