Because if the Builder has an end in mind, then everything He does today must serve that end.

A father preparing a son for adulthood trains differently than a father preparing a son for farming.

A king preparing heirs trains differently than a carpenter preparing apprentices.

**The future determines the present.**

---

# Perhaps We've Been Asking The Wrong Question

Instead of:

> What is discipleship?

Maybe the better question is:

> What is Christ preparing His people for?

Because only then can we understand:

- why He gives gifts,
- why He allows suffering,
- why He uses shepherds,
- why He commands perseverance,
- why holiness matters,
- why church life matters,
- why generations matter.
