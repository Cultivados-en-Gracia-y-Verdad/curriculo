# Paul's Interpretation

This is where things become really interesting.

### 1 Corinthians 3

Paul asks:

> What then is Apollos?
> 
> What is Paul?

His answer:

> servants.

Then:

> God was causing the growth.

---

Notice the structure.

```
Servants work       ↓God grows
```

Not:

```
Servants grow
```


Matthew may already be answering:

Not by absence.

But by presence.

# This Connects To John 15

Now suddenly John's language makes more sense.

### John 15

> Abide in Me.

Why?

Because Jesus is not merely a historical teacher whose lessons are remembered.

He remains actively involved.

Matthew repeatedly places Christ at the center of the action:

- He calls.
- He makes.
- He builds.
- He remains present.
- ---
- Ephesians 4 suggests:

```
Christ↓gives servants↓servants equip↓saints participate↓body grows↓Christ remains the source
```

That is remarkably consistent with:

- Matthew 16 ("I will build")
- Matthew 28 ("make disciples")
- Acts 14 ("make disciples... strengthen")
- Acts 19 (daily teaching)
- 1 Corinthians 3 ("God gives growth")

For the first time, I feel like we're beginning to see a coherent framework emerge rather than isolated observations. The next question I would ask is:

> **What does "equip" actually look like in Paul's ministry?**

Because if Ephesians 4 describes the mechanism Christ uses to build His church, then Acts and Timothy may show us that mechanism in action.
