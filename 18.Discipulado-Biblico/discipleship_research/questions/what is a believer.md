# What is a believer?

## Question

How does the New Testament define a believer?

## Why This Question Matters

The category "believer" is often assumed to be identical to:

- disciple
    
- Christian
    
- son of God
    

The relationship between these categories should be established from the text.

## Initial Observations

### John's Gospel

John's stated purpose is belief.

John 20:31

> These have been written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing you may have life in His name.

Observation:

John states his purpose in terms of believing.

### Acts

Acts frequently refers to believers.

Question:

How does Luke distinguish believers from disciples?

### Paul's Letters

Paul regularly uses belief language.

Examples:

- Romans
    
- Galatians
    
- Ephesians
    

Observation:

Belief remains a dominant category after disciple language disappears.
