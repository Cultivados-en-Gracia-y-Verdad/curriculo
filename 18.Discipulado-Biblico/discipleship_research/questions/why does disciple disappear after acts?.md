# Why does disciple disappear after Acts?

## Observation

The noun μαθητής (disciple) appears throughout the Gospels and Acts.

It does not appear in Romans through Revelation.

## Question

Why does the New Testament stop using disciple language after Acts?

## Possibilities To Investigate

### Possibility 1

The concept disappears.

### Possibility 2

The concept continues under different vocabulary.

Examples:

- believer
    
- saint
    
- son
    
- child
    
- brother
    
- those in Christ
    

### Possibility 3

Disciple is primarily a Gospel-Acts category describing people in relation to Jesus' earthly ministry.

### Possibility 4

Disciple remains important but is no longer the primary identity category used by the apostles.

## Evidence Needed

- Trace believer language.
    
- Trace sonship language.
    
- Trace "in Christ" language.
    
- Trace saint language.
