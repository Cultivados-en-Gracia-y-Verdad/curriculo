# What changed after Pentecost?

## Observations

### Before Pentecost

- Disciple language dominates.
- Jesus physically present.
- Follow Me.
- My disciples.

### After Pentecost

- Disciple language gradually declines.
- Spirit language increases.
- In Christ language increases.
- Sonship language increases.
- Church language increases.

## Questions

- Are these connected?
- Is the vocabulary shift related to new covenant realities?
- Is disciple a transitional category?
- Why do the Epistles prefer other identity categories?
