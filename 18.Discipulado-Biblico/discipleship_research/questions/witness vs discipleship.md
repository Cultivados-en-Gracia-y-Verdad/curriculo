# Witness vs Disciple-Maker

## Question

Are the biblical roles of witness and disciple-maker identical?

Or does Scripture distinguish between them?

## Observations

### John the Baptist

John 1:7

> He came as a witness.

Purpose:

> so that all might believe through him.

John points beyond himself.

---

### Samaritan Woman

John 4:39

> Many believed because of the word of the woman.

The woman points beyond herself.

---

### Moses

John 5:46

> If you believed Moses, you would believe Me.

Moses points beyond himself.

---

### Paul

1 Corinthians 3:5

> Servants through whom you believed.

Paul points beyond himself.

---

## Emerging Pattern

Witness

↓

Belief

↓

Christ

## Questions

- Is witness the primary role of God's servants?
    
- Is disciple-making accomplished through witness?
    
- Are these distinct concepts?
    
- How does Matthew 28:19 fit into this pattern?
