# John 8:30-31 - Belief and True Discipleship

## Text

John 8:30

> many came to believe in Him

John 8:31

> If you continue in My word, then you are truly disciples of Mine

## Observation

Jesus addresses people described as believers.

He then introduces continuing in His word.

Continuing is connected to being truly His disciples.

## Relationships Observed

Believe

↓

Continue in My word

↓

Truly My disciples

## Questions

- Why does Jesus add the condition of continuing?
    
- What is the relationship between belief and discipleship?
    
- Does John distinguish initial belief from continuing?
