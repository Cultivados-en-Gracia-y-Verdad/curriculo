# A Pattern Is Emerging

The servant seems unable to perform the crucial internal work.

For example:

### Paul

Can preach.

Cannot open Lydia's heart.

---

### Apollos

Can water.

Cannot give growth.

---

### Eli

Can instruct Samuel.

Cannot call Samuel.

---

### John the Baptist

Can point.

Cannot become the object of following.

---

This pattern keeps recurring.


### Disciples are told:

```
GoBaptizeTeach
```

These all fit the servant-side list.

---

Then Jesus says:

> I am with you always.

Why end with that?

Possibly because the crucial work remains His.

The servants can:

- go
- teach
- baptize

But Christ remains present to accomplish what only Christ can accomplish.
