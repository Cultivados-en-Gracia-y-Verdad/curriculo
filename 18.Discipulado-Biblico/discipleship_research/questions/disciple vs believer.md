# Disciple vs Believer

## Question

Are the categories "disciple" and "believer" identical in the New Testament?

Or does Scripture distinguish between them?

## Why This Question Matters

Many modern discussions assume:

Disciple = Believer

Before accepting that assumption, the text should be examined.

## Evidence Suggesting Distinction

### John 2:11

> His disciples believed in Him.

Observation:

The text speaks of disciples believing.

The categories appear distinguishable in this verse.

### John 6:66

> Many of His disciples withdrew and were not walking with Him anymore.

Observation:

People called disciples later stop following.

Question:

Does this affect the relationship between disciple and believer?

### John 8:31

> If you continue in My word, then you are truly disciples of Mine.

Observation:

Jesus qualifies discipleship with "truly."

Question:

Why is qualification necessary?

## Evidence Suggesting Overlap

### Acts

The followers of Jesus are frequently called disciples.

Acts 6:1

Acts 9:10

Acts 11:26

Observation:

Disciple appears to function as the normal designation for members of the Jesus movement.
