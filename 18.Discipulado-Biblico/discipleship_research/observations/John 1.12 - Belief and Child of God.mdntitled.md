# John 1:12 - Belief and Child of God

## Text

> But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, even to those who believe in His name.

## Observation

John directly connects belief with becoming children of God.

## Relationships Observed

Receive Him

↓

Believe in His name

↓

Become children of God

## Questions

- Are receiving and believing parallel expressions?
    
- What does John mean by becoming children of God?
    
- Does every believer become a child of God?
    
- How does this relate to discipleship?
