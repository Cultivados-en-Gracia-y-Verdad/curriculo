When Jesus says:

### Matthew 16:18

> I will build My church.

The future tense is striking.

The church is still future.

But disciples already exist.

That means, at minimum:

```
Disciples
```

and

```
Church
```

are not identical categories in Matthew 16.

The disciples exist.

The church is still something Jesus will build.

That is a fascinating observation and one we should definitely keep in front of us.


Which suddenly makes sense if Christ is the Builder.

The Builder remains.

The workers change.

The generations change.

But the house continues.
