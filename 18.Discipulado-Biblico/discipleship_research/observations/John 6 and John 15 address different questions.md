# John 6 and John 15 address different questions

## John 6

Major emphasis:

- come
- believe
- receive Jesus

Problem:

Will people accept Jesus for who He is?

Result:

Some leave.

Some remain.

---

## John 15

Major emphasis:

- abide
- fruit
- commandments
- love

Problem:

How does fruitfulness occur?

---

## Observation

John 6 and John 15 emphasize different categories.

John 6 emphasizes belief.

John 15 emphasizes abiding.

Observation only.
