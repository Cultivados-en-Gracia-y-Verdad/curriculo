The focus isn't on preserving Paul.

The focus is on preparing Timothy.

Paul is disappearing.

Timothy is the concern.

And even Timothy isn't the end.

Timothy prepares faithful men.

Faithful men prepare others.

The attention keeps moving forward.

---

### Think about 2 Timothy.

Paul knows his departure is near.

What is his concern?

Not:

- Remember me.
- Preserve my ministry.
- Maintain my organization.

But:

> The things which you heard from me... entrust to faithful men who will be able to teach others also.

Paul's eyes are on people he will never meet.

That's remarkable.
