# John emphasizes believing more than believers

## Observation

John frequently uses the verb "believe" (πιστεύω).

John rarely uses belief as a static identity label.

## Examples

John 1

John 3

John 5

John 6

John 11

John 20:31

## Question

Is belief being presented as an ongoing response to Jesus rather than merely a category?
