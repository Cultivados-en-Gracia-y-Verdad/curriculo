# John's stated purpose is belief

## Observation

John explicitly states the purpose of his Gospel.

John 20:31

> These have been written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing you may have life in His name.

## Significance

Throughout the Gospel, John frequently discusses disciples.

However, his stated purpose is expressed in terms of belief.
