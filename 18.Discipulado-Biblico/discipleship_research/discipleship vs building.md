## La visión debe permanecer en el Constructor

La iglesia necesita participar en la formación de personas.

Sin embargo, el centro no puede ser el discipulado mismo.

Cuando el discipulado se convierte en la visión, las generaciones posteriores terminan preservando métodos, programas, o tradiciones.

Pero cuando la visión permanece en el Constructor, todo encuentra su lugar.

Cristo está edificando Su iglesia.

Cristo tiene un propósito.

Cristo tiene una meta.

Cristo permanece con Su pueblo hasta el final del siglo.

El discipulado no constituye la visión.

El discipulado forma parte de la obra del Constructor.

Por lo tanto, cada generación debe transmitir algo más grande que sí misma.

No debe transmitir simplemente un método.

Debe transmitir la visión del Constructor.

Porque los obreros pasan.

Las generaciones cambian.

Pero el Constructor permanece.

Y Su propósito continúa hasta que Su casa esté terminada.

## The Danger of a Discipleship Vision

This may sound strange, but I think it deserves serious consideration.

Suppose a church says:

> "Our vision is discipleship."

What happens after one generation?

The next generation asks:

> Why discipleship?

And if discipleship itself becomes the vision, eventually it loses its center.

People begin reproducing:

- methods
- mentors
- programs
- culture

rather than Christ's vision.

---

## But the Builder's Vision Can Be Transmitted

Paul doesn't tell Timothy:

> Make sure people keep Paul's ministry alive.

Nor:

> Make sure discipleship survives.

The concern is always larger.

Christ's work.

Christ's gospel.

Christ's church.

Christ's appearing.

Christ's kingdom.

Timothy almost disappears into something bigger than himself.


**The church dies without generational transmission.**

But it also dies if the transmission becomes centered on itself.

The center cannot be:

```
discipleship
```

The center must be:

```
Christ↓His purpose↓His house↓His completion
```

And perhaps discipleship is not the vision.

Perhaps discipleship is one of the Builder's means of preserving and advancing His vision from generation to generation until the Bride is ready.

That perspective seems to organize everything we've discovered so far.
