# John - Belief Trace

## Purpose Statement

### John 20:31

> These have been written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing you may have life in His name.

Observation:

John explicitly states that his Gospel was written to produce belief.

Question:

What does John mean by believing?

---

## John 1:7

> so that all might believe through him.

Observation:

John the Baptist's testimony aims at belief.

Pattern:

Witness → Belief

---

## John 1:12

> But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, even to those who believe in His name.

Observation:

Belief is directly connected to becoming children of God.

Question:

What is the relationship between believer and child of God?

---

## John 2:11

> His disciples believed in Him.

Observation:

The text distinguishes disciples and believing.

Question:

Are disciple and believer identical categories?

---

## John 3:16

> whoever believes in Him shall not perish, but have eternal life.

Observation:

Belief is connected to eternal life.

---

## John 3:18

> He who believes in Him is not judged.

Observation:

Belief is connected to freedom from judgment.

---

## John 3:36

> He who believes in the Son has eternal life.

Observation:

Belief is connected to possessing eternal life.

---

## Open Questions

- What exactly is belief in John?
    
- Can belief be temporary?
    
- Is belief primarily intellectual, relational, or both?
    
- How does belief relate to discipleship?
    
- How does belief relate to becoming a child of God?
