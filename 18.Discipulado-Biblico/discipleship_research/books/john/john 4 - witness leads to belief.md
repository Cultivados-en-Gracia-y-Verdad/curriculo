# John 4 - Witness leads to belief

## Observation

The Samaritans initially believe because of the testimony of the woman.

John 4:39

> Many believed because of the word of the woman.

Later:

John 4:41

> Many more believed because of His word.

John 4:42

> We have heard for ourselves.

## Pattern

Witness

↓

Belief

↓

Personal encounter with Jesus

↓

Conviction
