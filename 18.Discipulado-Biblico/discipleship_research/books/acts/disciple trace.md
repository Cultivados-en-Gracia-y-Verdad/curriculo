# Acts Disciple Trace

## Acts 6:1

> while the disciples were increasing in number

### Observation

Luke uses **disciple** as a population category.

The focus is numerical growth.

Interesting:

Luke does not say:

> the church was increasing

He says:

> the disciples were increasing.

---

## Acts 6:2

> the congregation of the disciples

### Observation

A gathered body of disciples exists.

This sounds almost corporate.

Interesting because:

- disciples are individual followers
- yet Luke speaks of them collectively

---

## Acts 6:7

> the number of the disciples continued to increase greatly

### Observation

Word of God

↓

Disciples increase

This should be compared later with Matthew 28 and Acts 14.

---

# Acts 9

## Acts 9:1

> threats against the disciples of the Lord

### Observation

The defining feature is:

```
disciplesof the Lord
```

Not disciples of apostles.

Not disciples of a church.

Not disciples of Peter.

---

## Acts 9:10

> a disciple at Damascus named Ananias

### Observation

A normal believer is called a disciple.

No leadership office is implied.

---

## Acts 9:19

> he spent several days with the disciples

### Observation

The local community is described as disciples.

---

## Acts 9:26

> he tried to associate with the disciples

### Observation

Again the community is identified as disciples.

---

# Acts 11

## Acts 11:26

> the disciples were first called Christians in Antioch

This may be the most important verse in the trace.

### Observation

Luke's categories:

```
disciples      ↓calledChristians
```

Disciple appears to be the primary internal designation.

Christian appears as a secondary label.

---

# Acts 13–14

## Acts 14:21

> preached the gospel
> 
> and made many disciples

One of the most important verses in the entire project.

### Observation

Luke directly connects:

```
Gospel    ↓Make disciples
```

This is the clearest disciple-making statement in Acts.

---

## Acts 14:22

> strengthening the souls of the disciples

### Observation

Making disciples is immediately followed by strengthening disciples.

Interesting distinction.

Luke does not treat them as identical actions.

---

# Acts 15

## Acts 15:10

> placing upon the neck of the disciples a yoke

### Observation

The Jerusalem council speaks about disciples.

Not church members.

Not Christians.

Disciples.

---

# Acts 16

## Acts 16:1

> a disciple was there, named Timothy

This one should go on the board immediately.

### Observation

Timothy is called a disciple.

This becomes interesting later because Paul never calls Timothy a disciple in his letters.

---

# Acts 18

## Acts 18:23

> strengthening all the disciples

Again:

```
disciples      ↓strengthened
```

Recurring pattern.

---

## Acts 18:27

> he greatly helped those who had believed

Interesting.

Same chapter.

Luke uses:

```
disciples
```

and

```
those who had believed
```

This deserves attention.

# Acts 19

## Acts 19:1

> he found some disciples

Very important.

Luke can identify people as disciples before determining whether they understand the gospel correctly.

That should remain on our board.
