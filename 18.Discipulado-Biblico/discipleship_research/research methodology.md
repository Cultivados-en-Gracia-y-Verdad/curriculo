# Research Methodology

## Rule 1

Keep categories separate until Scripture joins them.

## Rule 2

Do not define a word from a lexicon before tracing its usage by the author.

## Rule 3

Do not build conclusions from isolated verses.

Trace the category across the author's writings.

## Rule 4

Questions generate observations.

Observations generate hypotheses.

Hypotheses must be tested before becoming findings.

## Rule 5

Ask what problem the author is addressing before asking what a word means.
