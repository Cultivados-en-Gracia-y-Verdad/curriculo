# Abide Trace

## Research Question

How does John use "abide" (μένω) across his writings?

## Gospel of John

### John 8:31

> If you continue in My word, then you are truly disciples of Mine.

Observation:

Continuing is connected with true discipleship.

---

### John 15:4

> Abide in Me.

Observation:

Abiding is commanded.

---

### John 15:5

> He who abides in Me and I in him bears much fruit.

Observation:

Fruit is connected to abiding.

---

### John 15:7

> If you abide in Me and My words abide in you...

Observation:

The relationship is mutual.

---

### John 15:9

> Abide in My love.

Observation:

Abiding is connected to love.

## Questions

- What is abiding?
    
- Is abiding different from believing?
    
- Why does John emphasize abiding so strongly?
    
- How does abiding relate to discipleship?
