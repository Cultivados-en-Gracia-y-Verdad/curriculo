# A Pattern Is Becoming Difficult To Ignore

Across Scripture:

|Servant|God|
|---|---|
|Calls attention|Calls people|
|Speaks God's word|Opens hearts|
|Teaches|Gives understanding|
|Plants|Gives growth|
|Encourages|Sustains|
|Strengthens|Gives life|
|Points|Remains the destination|

Notice how often the servant's role is mediating rather than originating.
