# Introduction

## The Tension

"We have questions."

Not answers.

# Chapter 1

## Let's Begin

Where do we begin?

"Make disciples."

Questions multiply.

Another statement emerges:

> I will build My church.

Which produces another question:

# Chapter 2

## The Builder

Not activity.

Identity.

Ownership.

Design.

Purpose.

Whose project is this?

And once we establish that Christ is the Builder, another question naturally arises:

# Chapter 3

## Who Is Building?

Tension:

-   Is everything God's work?
-   Is everything man's work?
-   Why command men if Christ builds?
-   What do servants actually do?

1 Corinthians 3 becomes central.

Roles remain distinct.

And then:

# Chapter 4

## What Is The Builder Building?

(Ephesians 2, 4, 5; Romans 8)

Now we have enough data to ask:

-   One body?
-   One new man?
-   Household?
-   Temple?
-   Dwelling?
-   Bride?
-   Mature man?
-   Conformed to Christ?

And I suspect Chapter 5 becomes:

# How Does The Builder Build?

Not:

How do we make disciples?

But:

How does the Builder employ servants?

-   Matthew 28
-   Acts
-   Ephesians 4
-   2 Timothy 2

Then perhaps:

# Chapter 6

## Where Does The Builder Build?

And suddenly we're asking:

Is the church merely where disciples gather?

Or is church life itself the God-designed environment for growth?

Ephesians 4.

One another passages.

Household language.

Body language.

Then:

# Chapter 7

## What Is The Builder's Goal?

Not:

Get decisions.

Not:

Multiply churches.

Not:

Finish curriculum.

But:

What does the finished work look like?

-   Fullness of Christ?
-   Bride prepared?
-   Conformed to His image?
-   Sharing His glory?
-   Reign?

And only after all that do we finally ask:

# Chapter 8

## So What Is Discipleship?

Which is almost humorous.

Because we started there.

But now we're asking it after:

-   Who owns the project?
-   Who is building?
-   What is being built?
-   How does He build?
-   Where does He build?
-   What is His goal?

And I suspect by then, "discipleship" won't be some separate ministry.

It may simply be:

> Human participation in what Christ the Builder is accomplishing until the end of the age.
