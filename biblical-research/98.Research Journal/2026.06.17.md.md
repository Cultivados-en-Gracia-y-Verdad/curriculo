## Major Insight

The research should not begin with discipleship as the center.

The stronger organizing question is:

> How does Christ build His church through human servants until the end of the age?

## Important Observations

- Matthew 16:18 explicitly identifies Christ as the Builder.
- Matthew 28:20 extends His presence until the end of the age.
- 1 Corinthians 3 distinguishes servant work from God's growth.
- 2 Timothy 2:2 shows generational transmission.
- Ephesians 4 gives an "until" connected to body maturity and fullness of Christ.

## Active Concern

Discipleship becomes unclear when isolated from the Builder's work.

## New Insight

Ephesians 2 describes what Christ is building almost entirely through corporate images:

- one new man
- one body
- household

The emphasis falls on a corporate reality rather than isolated individuals.
