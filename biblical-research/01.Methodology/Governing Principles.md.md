# Governing Principles

## Scripture above all

Scripture governs the laboratory.

## The laboratory governs systems

Systems do not govern the laboratory.

## Research precedes manuals

Manuals are outputs.

## Observations before conclusions

Never force synthesis prematurely.

## Drift must be documented

Errors are assets.

## Questions are welcome

Unresolved questions are not failures.

## Work quickly.

## Work logically.

## Work patiently.
