# Error 001 Defining discipleship apart from the Builder

## Error

We tried to define discipleship directly without first identifying what Christ is building.

## Result

The study became circular and fragmented.

Questions multiplied without producing clarity:

- Is discipleship teaching?
    
- Is discipleship mentoring?
    
- Is discipleship evangelism?
    
- Is every believer making disciples?
    
- Is discipleship church life?
    

## Correction

Begin with the Builder.

Primary question:

> What is Christ building?

Then ask:

> How do servants participate in His work?

## Status

Drift correction accepted.
