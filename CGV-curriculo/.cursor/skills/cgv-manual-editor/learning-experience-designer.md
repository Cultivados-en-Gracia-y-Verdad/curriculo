# Skill 4 — Learning Experience Designer ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

Evaluates whether the page teaches the reader how to read, not just what the text says.

## Job

A CGV manual is a **reading guide**.

After the page, the student should be better at *following an author* — not only richer in facts about this verse.

## Pass / fail

| Pass | Fail |
|---|---|
| Student learns a reading move (watch the subject shift; hold the “si…”; track the word that returns) | Student only receives a conclusion |
| Mix of observation / pressure / connection / short explanation | One monotonous pattern for every clause |
| Major movements get more care than transitions | Flat density — everything equal |
| Next paragraph is easier to read because of this page | Page is a closed lesson that does not hand off |

## Checklist

- [ ] After this comment, does the student see something they probably would have missed?
- [ ] Does the page teach *how* to watch the author — or only *what* we decided the line means?
- [ ] Is attention weighted (major moves > transitions)?
- [ ] Is there a healthy mix of comment kinds (not one template)?
- [ ] Would this help me read the next paragraph better?

## Design moves

1. Prefer observation and pressure over explanation.
2. Put weight on turns: subject change, purpose opened, contrast, delay, recurring word.
3. Let small seams breathe with less commentary.
4. Keep one primary purpose per `>`.

## Success line

The student becomes a better reader of *this author* — not a consumer of editorial conclusions.
