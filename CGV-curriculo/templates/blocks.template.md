## Inventory

Repeat one block per unit, in document order. The blocks must **tile the book** — each ends the
verse before the next begins, the last reaches the final verse.

### {ref} — {form named in the text's own vocabulary} {n de N, if in a series}

- **Boundary evidence** — the formula or marker that opens it, quoted from LBF with its reference.
  If the boundary rests on something other than a formula (a change of addressee that persists, a
  date, a closing doxology), say which.
- **Form** — from the text's own words. See spec.md §3 for the naming rule.
- **Constituent parts** — the sub-moves the unit itself makes, if it has a repeating internal shape
  (vision proper → question → interpretation; charge → response → verdict).
- **Contenido** — what happens, or what is asserted, in the text's own referents.

  Governed by the drift tests. It must be contradictable by pointing at this passage alone. No
  cause, no significance, no application, no cross-book theology. **Where the text leaves a tension
  open, the statement leaves it open** (Constitution §5.2).

- **Cláusulas** — the clause IDs that warrant the statement above. A statement with no IDs is not a
  statement, it is an impression (Constitution §5.5).
