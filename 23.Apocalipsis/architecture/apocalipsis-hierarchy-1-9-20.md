## Movements (five)

### 1. Juan en Patmos; en el Espíritu oye la voz que manda · locates 1:9–11
- Roots / clusters: c32 · c38 ‖ c40 · c43 · c47 ‖ c48
- Nesting: c32→c33→c34, c35, c36‖c37; c38→c39; c40→c41→c42 (host open); c43→c44,c45; c47←c46; c48→c49,c50
- Continuity: one first-person chain — Juan names himself and where he was (`ἐγενόμην`), then the same verb again (*en el Espíritu*), then hearing opens speech and the double command. No discourse cut between isla and Espíritu. Speaker of *Yo soy* stays **unnamed** (a voice).

### 2. Se vuelve y ve · locates 1:12–13
- Roots / clusters: c51 ‖ c55
- Nesting: c51→c52→c53; c55←c54; c55→c56→c57‖c58
- Continuity: Juan turns *to see the voice*; what he sees is lampstands and, in their midst, one like a son of man (verse cut 12→13 is not a clause cut).

### 3. Cómo es el que está en medio · locates 1:14–16
- Roots / clusters: c59 ‖ c61 ‖ c62 ‖ c64 ‖ c66 ‖ c67 (+ c65 open)
- Nesting: c59→c60; c62→c63; c67→c68; c65 held under the series with unresolved host
- Continuity: one descriptive accumulation about the one just seen (`αὐτοῦ`), not six verse fragments. Six nominal predications (plus the open participle) stay peers under one movement.

### 4. Juan cae; la figura pone la mano y habla · locates 1:17–18
- Roots / clusters: c70 ‖ c72 · c74 · c75 ‖ c77 ‖ c79 ‖ c81 · c80
- Nesting: c70←c69, →c71; c72→c73→c74; c75→c76; c79←c78
- Continuity: only explicit inter-clause time in the span (`ὅτε`); then the seen figure acts (subject unexpressed) and speaks through *Amén* and the keys. *Yo soy* here is spoken by the one Juan sees — not a silent rename of 1:11’s voice.

### 5. Escribe; el misterio se abre hacia las iglesias · locates 1:19–20
- Roots / clusters: c82 · c90 ‖ c91
- Nesting: c82→c83‖c84‖c85→c86, c87→c88, c89; c91→c92
- Continuity: mandate resumes (same γράψον thread as 1:11); objects include the mystery (read as object of *Escribe*, not a free nominative header); the two `εἰσιν` declarations explain what was seen and must be written — same movement, not a new H2.
